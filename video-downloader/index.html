Privacy Policy — Video Downloader

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Video Downloader ("the extension") is a browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox that lists the videos on the web page you are viewing and saves the ones you choose to your computer, with a download queue to manage them. Your privacy is fundamental to how it is built.

1. Data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, sell, share, or send your personal data or browsing activity to the developer or to anyone else. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, trackers, or developer servers of any kind.

2. What is stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your own device using the browser's storage API, and is removed if you uninstall the extension:

  • The list of videos found on each open tab (cleared when the tab closes).
  • Your download queue: titles, addresses, progress and status of downloads.
  • A download log of up to 500 entries: page address, file name, size, and the reason a download failed. You can clear it any time under Settings → Download log.
  • Your settings: save folder, file name pattern, quality and downloads at a time. If you use your browser's sync, the browser may sync these settings between your own devices.

3. Connections the extension makes

  • The sites you visit and download from, to check a video's type and size and to download the video you chose.
  • YouTube's public oEmbed service, on YouTube pages only, to read a video's title.
  • The optional Video Downloader Helper app on your own Windows computer, when you download something that needs it (videos that play in pieces, and social video posts). The extension passes it the address of the video or page you chose. The helper downloads it from that site with the open-source tool yt-dlp, keeps its own log in its install folder on your computer, and checks GitHub for yt-dlp updates at most once a week.

None of these connections send your data to the developer.

4. Permissions

  • downloads — save the videos you choose and show their progress in the queue.
  • storage — keep your settings, the queue and the download log on your device.
  • tabs — show the videos found on the page you are viewing.
  • notifications — tell you when a download finishes.
  • contextMenus — add "Download this video" to the right-click menu.
  • nativeMessaging — talk to the optional helper app on your computer.
  • access to all websites — videos can be on any site; the extension looks for them only on the pages you visit.

5. Third parties

The extension does not use third-party analytics or advertising services and does not send data to anyone. The helper app uses yt-dlp and FFmpeg, which run on your own computer.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date.
