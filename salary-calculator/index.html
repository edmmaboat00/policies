Salary Calculator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Salary Calculator is a payroll/tax calculator that runs entirely inside your browser. Your privacy is simple to describe: the extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

What data the extension handles

Everything you type — salary figures, hours, rates and tax bands — is processed locally on your own device to compute the results shown on screen. None of it is sent to us or to any third party. There are no analytics, no trackers, and no external network requests.

Local storage

The extension uses your browser's local storage (the storage permission) to remember your last inputs and your edited rate settings between sessions, purely for your convenience. This data stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the extension or click “Reset”.

The “tabs” permission

The tabs permission is used for one purpose only: when you click the toolbar icon, the extension opens (or re-focuses) its own calculator tab. It does not read, monitor, or collect your browsing history or the content of any other tab.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use any data for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending. Because no data ever leaves your device, there is nothing to share.
