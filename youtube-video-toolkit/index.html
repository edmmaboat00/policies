Privacy Policy for YouTube Video Toolkit

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how YouTube Video Toolkit (formerly ClearView) handles information when you use the browser extension.

Information handling

YouTube Video Toolkit does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not operate a backend service, include analytics or advertising trackers, or sell or share user information.

The extension processes information on supported YouTube pages locally in your browser to provide the features you choose. This includes video playback state, player controls, page elements, and video titles used to name screenshots and recordings. This information is not sent to the developer.

AI summaries, transcripts and questions

The AI summary panel reads the captions (transcript), title, channel name and description of the video you are watching. This happens only for the video you are viewing.

When you ask for a summary or ask a question, that video's transcript and details, plus your question, are sent to the AI engine you have chosen, and only to it:

  • On-device AI (Chrome's built-in model): processed on your computer; nothing leaves the browser.
  • Gemini, OpenAI or Claude API with your own key: sent directly from your browser to that provider (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic) under your own account and that provider's privacy policy. The extension asks for permission to reach a provider only when you save its key. In Firefox it also asks you to agree to sharing website content.
  • Copy and open ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini (no key): the prompt is copied to your clipboard and the chosen site opens in a new tab. Nothing is sent until you paste it there yourself.

Nothing is sent to the developer. Summaries are not generated automatically unless you turn on "Summarize automatically".

Settings stored on your device

The extension uses your browser's local extension storage to save preferences, such as playback speed, preferred quality, audio settings, themes, player layout, distraction filters, and keyboard shortcuts. These preferences remain in the browser profile where the extension is installed and are not uploaded by the extension.

AI API keys you enter, and the summaries, notes and conversations you save, are also kept in local extension storage in this browser. You can delete them from the AI summary settings and the Saved summaries page. Removing the extension deletes them.

You can reset preferences in the extension's settings. You can also export settings to a file or import a settings file you select. Exported files remain wherever you choose to save them.

Screenshots, clipboard, and recordings

Screenshots and recordings are created locally when you request them. Depending on your settings, screenshots are saved to your device, copied to your clipboard, or both. Recordings capture the video player's content and available playback audio; the extension does not request camera or microphone access.

Recording data is held in memory during recording and saved locally when recording stops. The extension does not upload screenshots, recordings, or clipboard contents. Other applications may be able to access your clipboard according to your device's settings.

Files you save or export remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the extension does not delete these files or clear your clipboard.

Permissions and website access

  • Storage: Saves and restores your extension preferences, AI keys and saved summaries locally.
  • Scripting: Adds the AI summary panel to YouTube tabs that were already open when the extension was installed or updated.
  • Optional access to generativelanguage.googleapis.com, api.openai.com and api.anthropic.com: Requested only when you save an API key for that provider, so the extension can send it the transcript you want summarized.
  • Access to www.youtube.com and www.youtube-nocookie.com: Allows the extension to customize supported YouTube pages and embedded players, including playback, audio, appearance, and distraction controls.

All extension code is included in the installed package. The extension does not download or execute remotely hosted code.

Third-party services

YouTube continues to operate under its own privacy policy. If you use an AI provider with your own key, or paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, that provider's privacy policy applies to what you send it. This policy covers YouTube Video Toolkit only; it does not describe information collected by YouTube, your browser, or other installed extensions.

Your choices

You can change or disable features in the extension's settings and disable or uninstall the extension through your browser's extension manager. You can delete saved screenshots, recordings, and exported settings using your device's file manager. You can remove AI keys in the AI summary settings and delete saved summaries, notes and chats on the Saved summaries page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact
